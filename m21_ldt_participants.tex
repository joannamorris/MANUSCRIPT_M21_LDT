\subsection*{Participants}

We tested 76 participants recruited from the UMass--Five Colleges consortium
community in Amherst, MA between September 2017 and May 2019. All procedures were
approved by the Institutional Review Board at Hampshire College (protocol title:
``Behavioral and ERP Studies of Language Comprehension''; initial approval
February 17, 2016, with extensions approved through February 2019). No protocol
number was assigned. Participants were healthy adult volunteers who provided
written informed consent prior to participation. Identifying information was not
collected, and all data were analysed in de-identified form. This study was
conducted prior to the widespread adoption of preregistration norms in cognitive
neuroscience; no preregistration was completed.

Participants were eligible if they were between the ages of 18 and 26,
right-handed, native English speakers with no other languages spoken in the home
before age 3, and reported normal or corrected-to-normal vision with no history
of cognitive or reading-related difficulties. Participants were compensated for
their time.

Twenty participants were excluded. Six did not meet the eligibility criteria:
three were older than 26, two were not native English speakers and one reported
a language disability. For ten,
data were incomplete: four had no usable EEG recording; five had incomplete
language-test data, so their Orthographic Sensitivity and Language Proficiency
scores could not be computed; and for one, no behavioural responses were
recorded, so trials could not be scored. Of the remaining participants, one made
no responses during the lexical decision task, and three performed close to
chance ($d' < 0.5$; see Statistical analysis).

The final sample comprised 56 participants (41 female, 14 male, one did not
report their sex), aged 18--25 years ($M = 19.6$, $SD = 1.6$). Forty-four
identified as White, six as more than one race, four as Black and one as Asian;
three identified as Hispanic or Latino. One participant did not report race or
ethnicity. Race and ethnicity were collected by self-report on a demographic
questionnaire administered at the start of the session.
No socioeconomic data were collected for this sample.
